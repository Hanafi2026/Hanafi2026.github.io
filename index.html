About Me

I am Hanafi Abdulkadri Oladipupo, an Electrical and Electronics Engineer, technology professional, and Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) awardee pursuing doctoral research in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Kyungpook National University (KNU), South Korea.

My work connects engineering practice with artificial intelligence. Having worked across power systems, radio-frequency spectrum monitoring, digital transformation, and information security, I am interested in developing intelligent technologies that solve practical problems and make critical systems safer, more reliable, and more efficient.

Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of intelligent systems and real-world engineering applications.

Current Doctoral Direction

I am exploring AI-driven condition monitoring and predictive maintenance for industrial generator systems, including how predictive models perform under real operating conditions and whether their results generalize to previously unseen machines.

My wider interests include secure IoT-enabled smart homes, trustworthy AI, and data-driven engineering decisions.

Professional Experience

I serve in Nigeria's Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy, where my engineering career has included RF spectrum monitoring, interference management, engineering maintenance, and digital-service modernization.

My experience includes work on enterprise content management and workflow automation, generator control and transfer systems, and maintenance of electrical and related infrastructure.

Research & Publication

My master's research examined wireless LAN security using the International Radio Monitoring Station in Gusau as a case study, focusing on practical network vulnerabilities and protective measures.

Hanafi, A. O., & Ahmad, B. I. (2019). “Analysis of Wireless Local Area Network Security: A Case Study of International Radio Monitoring Station (IRMS)-Gusau Wireless LAN.” Nigerian Journal of Scientific Research, 18(4).

Community & Service

Beyond research and engineering, I am committed to educational opportunity and community service. In 2019, I co-founded a charitable initiative supporting widows, orphans, and vulnerable families through community contributions.

I value cross-cultural exchange and the opportunity to connect knowledge gained in South Korea with practical needs in Nigeria and beyond.

My motivation: To apply engineering knowledge, artificial intelligence, and responsible innovation to real challenges—especially the reliability of industrial systems and the security of increasingly connected environments.
